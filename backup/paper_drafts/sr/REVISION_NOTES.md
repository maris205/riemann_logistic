# Revision Note / Summary of Changes

**Manuscript:** Spectral Isomorphism between Renormalization Flow in Non-Autonomous Quadratic Maps and Riemann Zeros
**Author:** Liang Wang

This note summarizes a self-initiated revision made while awaiting the
review. It addresses, proactively, the kinds of concerns most likely to be
raised about a manuscript that touches the Riemann Hypothesis, and brings the
framing in line with our companion (Hénon-map) study.

## Principal changes

1. **Connection to the primes is now anchored in a published result.**
   The introduction and abstract now build explicitly on
   Wang, *Research in Mathematics* 13(1), 2026 (the Logistic band-merging /
   prime-sieve isomorphism). The present paper is framed as the natural
   spectral follow-up: given a deterministic map tied to the arithmetic of the
   sieve, what spectrum does it carry, and how does it compare to the Riemann
   zeros? This removes the impression that the link to the zeros is asserted
   rather than motivated.

2. **Claims downgraded to match the evidence.** Proof-level and promotional
   language ("conclusively/definitively proves", "deterministic topological
   destiny", "perfect quantum locking", "shatters the RMT illusion",
   "spontaneous zeroing", etc.) has been removed throughout and replaced by
   "numerical observation", "heuristic", "qualitative", or "hypothesis", as
   appropriate.

3. **Random-matrix discussion corrected.** We no longer claim that RMT/GUE
   "fails". The text now states accurately that the GUE correspondence concerns
   the *unfolded local* statistics of the zeros, and that our globally scaled
   GUE comparison is an (asymmetric) counting-function comparison, not a
   refutation of RMT.

4. **USTC comparison downgraded to qualitative.** The spatial coincidence
   between the model's oscillation orders and the enlarged experimental
   deviations is now presented as a qualitative observation and an explicit
   *hypothesis*, not as proof of a shared physical mechanism; we note the
   missing significance test and uncertainty budget.

5. **Added a claim-classification table** (established / published / numerical /
   heuristic / qualitative / conjectural), including the explicit row
   "Operator spectrum equals the Riemann zeros — Not claimed (conjectural)".

6. **Format.** The manuscript has been typeset in the Scientific Reports
   LaTeX template; figures are proper floats with captions, references are in a
   `.bib` file, and the abstract is within length.

The numerical results, figures, and the released code/data are unchanged; only
the framing, claims, and presentation have been revised.
